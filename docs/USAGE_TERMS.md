# Intended use, ownership and redistribution

This collection documents research/challenge systems, not clinically validated
medical devices. Do not use generated reports or CT images for patient care
without appropriate independent validation and authorization.

No blanket permissive license is newly assigned here. Before making the source
or checkpoints public, the team must confirm ownership of its original code,
retain third-party notices, choose a license for code it owns, and comply with
all component/data/model licenses. In particular review DINOv2, Qwen2.5-0.5B,
the specific packaged RadBERT checkpoint/classifier, DistilBERT, MONAI and
CT-RATE/challenge terms. Local filenames do not determine redistribution rights.

Public weight URLs have not been supplied. The form should currently indicate
not available/access restricted, rather than selecting GitHub/Hugging Face
prematurely. Once authorized hosting is complete, link exact versioned archives
and retain their SHA-256. Task2 needs the complete generator/adapter/classifier/
reranker bundle, not an unrelated language-model base checkpoint.

Preserve uncertainty and negative findings. No Bayesian posterior-fusion,
synthetic-augmentation or diffusion results are claimed for these submissions.
AI assistance should be disclosed under the publication's policy; the human
authors remain responsible for verification, interpretation and final approval.
